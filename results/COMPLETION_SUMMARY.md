## 🎓 Metrics Updated to CV (%)

### Correction Applied

**From**: Absolute standard deviation values
**To**: Coefficient of Variation percentages

**Formula**: CV (%) = (Standard Deviation / Mean) × 100

**Example**:
```
Before: Energy_J_std = 0.000391 (hard to interpret)
After:  Energy CV = 85.3% (clearly shows variability)

Before: Score_std = 0.0272 (no context)
After:  Score CV = 131.6% (relative to mean score)
```

### Interpretation Guide

| CV (%) | Rating | Applicability |
|--------|--------|---|
| < 10% | Excellent | ✓ Real-time critical apps |
| 10-20% | Good | ✓ Standard deployments |
| 20-30% | Acceptable | ⚠ Monitor needed |
| > 30% | High | ❌ QoS-critical unsuitable |
